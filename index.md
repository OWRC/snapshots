# Introduction

**Snapshots** are a growing collection of interactive figures, maps, and data summaries developed by the Oak Ridges Moraine Groundwater Program (ORMGP). They provide an accessible way to explore and understand environmental conditions, patterns, and trends across our study area.

Drawing on the Program's extensive collection of hydrogeological, hydrological, and environmental data, these snapshots translate complex datasets into concise, visual information to support research, planning, and informed decision-making.

The collection is periodically updated and expanded as new data, analytical methods, and insights become available.

# About Us

The **Oak Ridges Moraine Groundwater Program (ORMGP)** is an arm's-length municipal initiative serving much of south-central Ontario, Canada.

Working collaboratively with 15 municipal partners, the Program collects, maintains, and analyzes regional groundwater and related environmental information. We provide shared datasets, scientific expertise, and analytical tools to support evidence-based water resource management.

Because groundwater systems extend beyond municipal boundaries, the Program emphasizes regional collaboration and a consistent scientific understanding of our shared water resources.
